## What the score means

This is the official result of the documentation pre-flight tool. It asks whether
Ekonerg's written QA controls cover the 18 Appendix B criteria, interpreted using
the approved NQA-1 Part I source. It does not certify field performance.

Keep the approved scale: fully 5/5 = 100; substantially 4/5 = 75;
partially 3/5 = 50; minimally 2/5 = 25; unmet 1/5 = 0.
Average the 18 equal-weight criterion scores and round half-up to one decimal.
These points are an ordinal summary, not a measured fraction of all clauses.
No category, weight, source edition or applicability ruling changes.

## Apply one fair rule to every criterion

- Fully: written controls cover the applicable duty, including who does what,
  how it is checked and what is recorded. Completed work samples may still be
  needed in the real audit.
- Substantially: the core written process is clear, but a bounded duty or control
  route needs clarification. Name that gap; do not just demand more documents.
- Partially: useful policy and some controls exist, but an important part of the
  method is not established by the supplied evidence.
- Minimally: only a small part of the duty has meaningful written support.
- Unmet: the applicable duty lacks meaningful written support, or a reviewed
  written rule directly prevents meeting it.

Read for purpose, not matching words. Combine related controls across chapters
and procedures. Credit a generic rule that requires a suitable project plan:
an absent completed project plan alone is not a defect in that generic rule.
Also distinguish a promised future method from a defined present process.
Procedure names, standards lists and crumb volume alone cannot earn full marks.

Ekonerg's main scope is engineering, design and related services. Do not assume
it manufactures every item, operates a nuclear plant, or performs every possible
special process. All 18 criteria stay applicable as approved. State the limits
of conditional hardware/process duties; do not silently mark them not applicable.
No blanket NQA-1 Part II obligation is introduced.

## Part 21: separate and still applicable

reporting path remains unresolved: PQ10.2-1 section 3.11 describes notification
to NEK, which is not by itself proof of the complete applicable reporting route.
The owner agrees to keep this issue visible but outside Criterion XVI and the
overall Appendix B score. Full XVI means its corrective-action policy is covered;
it does not mean full Part 21 compliance. XV carries the same boundary.
