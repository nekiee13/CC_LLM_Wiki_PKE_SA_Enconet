# Fair scoring of written QA controls

## What the score means

This tool checks Ekonerg's written QA system. The owner will check how it works
in a real audit. A full documentation match does not mean that field work has
already been checked. It means the supplied text covers the applicable duty.

Keep the approved five levels and points. Do not add categories or change the
numbers to get a nicer result.

| Level | Points | What it means for this document review |
|---|---:|---|
| Fully matched, 5/5 | 100 | Written controls cover the applicable duties. Work records can still need a real-audit check. |
| Substantially matched, 4/5 | 75 | The main controls exist. A limited but important written detail is unclear or missing. |
| Partially matched, 3/5 | 50 | Some useful controls exist, but a material part of the written method is missing or deferred. |
| Minimally matched, 2/5 | 25 | There is limited relevant control, not a usable method for the main duty. |
| Unmet, 1/5 | 0 | No relevant written control was found, or the supplied control conflicts with the duty. |

Use the meaning of the text, not a word-for-word match. Credit a clear control
even when it uses other words. A standard name or a procedure title alone is not
the procedure. More crumbs do not by themselves mean better compliance.

Check Appendix B against the supplied NQA-1 Part I. Keep Part II out of the
mandatory baseline. Apply item and process duties only to the work in scope;
do not assume Ekonerg does every kind of nuclear work. All 18 existing approved
applicability rulings remain in place.

Keep real written gaps visible. Missing work records alone are a check for the
real audit, not an automatic score loss. A wrong reference that makes the
controlling method unclear can still be a written gap. Do not turn every typo
into a failure of the whole QA system.

needed a separate check. That is not a missing corrective-action control under
Appendix B XVI or NQA-1 Part I Requirement 16, paragraph 100.

The full manual gives prompt action, cause analysis, prevention of repeat
problems, records, management reports and checks of completed actions:

- Manual 8.7: significant or repeated problems trigger a cause review.
- Manual 9.2: act without undue delay; check the action and report to management.
- Manual 10.2: find causes, prevent repeats, keep results and check success.
- Manual Prilog 2: track significant actions, their effect and management reports.
- PQ10.2-2 revision 10, sections 2.2, 2.5, 2.7 and 3.1-3.8: assign the cause,
  approval, follow-up, reopening and management-reporting duties.

The linked manual crumbs 0004, 0007, 0008 and 0010 under APP_B_XVI contain these
controls. Their quotes and full source chapters remain linked in the dashboard.
XVI can therefore be **fully matched for documentation: 100 points**.

The Part 21 question stays open for follow-up. PQ10.2-1 revision 10, section 3.11
sets evaluation and customer-notification steps. That does not by itself settle
all applicable reporting duties. This correction does not approve Part 21
compliance or remove its audit check.

The other 17 ratings stay as they were. Their reasons identify written detail
gaps, missing referenced methods or unresolved reference chains, not only missing
work samples. This pass does not claim that all those details have been re-sieved.
