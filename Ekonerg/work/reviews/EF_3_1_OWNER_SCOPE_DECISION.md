# EF-3.1 owner scope decision and preliminary applicability screen

**Date:** 2026-10-02  
**Status:** owner direction recorded; preliminary screen only; not a score or
conformity conclusion.

## 1. Audit question

The audit asks whether Ekonerg's quality-assurance system meets the 10 CFR 50
Appendix B benchmark, using ASME NQA-1 Part 1 to interpret the benchmark.
Ekonerg is the only supplier in this audit scope.

## 2. Activities in scope

The supplied Ekonerg QMS documents support these activities:

- design work;
- engineering services; and
- consultancy.

These are working scope descriptions inferred from the documents and the
owner's direction. They must be tested against real work samples.

### Supplier-scope note

The supplier boundary is vague. Ekonerg is the only supplier being audited,
but the supplied documents do not show which outside suppliers or
subcontractors Ekonerg uses for safety-related work, or which requirements are
flowed down to them. Supplier controls therefore remain in scope where Ekonerg
buys or subcontracts work that can affect quality. Ask for the supplier list,
sample contracts, and flow-down evidence before making a final N/A decision.

## 3. Part 21 scope

10 CFR Part 21 is applicable to this audit, mainly for:

- nonconformance identification and disposition; and
- corrective-action, cause, reporting, and follow-up controls.

It is not being used to add a second, unrelated audit question. Its role is to
test the relevant Ekonerg controls around nonconformances and corrective
actions.

## 4. ASME NQA-1 hierarchy

- **Part 1:** mandatory interpretation requirements for this audit baseline.
- **Part 2:** not mandatory by itself. Use a Part 2 subpart only when a
  contract, approved scope, or another explicit requirement invokes it.
- **Parts 3 and 4:** guidance/background unless separately invoked.

An ASME NQA-1 statement must not be treated as binding just because it appears
in the supplied standard. The audit record must state the reason for using any
Part 2, 3, or 4 material.

## 5. Preliminary Appendix B applicability screen

This screen is the next audit route, not a final N/A ruling. `Applicable` means
the described Ekonerg work normally needs the control. `Conditional` means the
control depends on a fact that must be checked in a work sample. No criterion
is marked final N/A from silence.

| Criterion | Preliminary state | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AB-01 Organization | Applicable | Design and consultancy need defined responsibility and independent QA authority. |
| AB-02 QA Program | Applicable | The audit examines Ekonerg's QA system itself. |
| AB-03 Design Control | Applicable | Design and engineering services are explicitly in scope. |
| AB-04 Procurement Document Control | Applicable | Ekonerg must control quality requirements in any purchase or subcontract. |
| AB-05 Instructions | Applicable | Engineering work needs approved methods, procedures, and acceptance criteria. |
| AB-06 Document Control | Applicable | Controlled design and consultancy documents are core outputs. |
| AB-07 Purchased Items | Conditional | Applies if Ekonerg buys items or services that can affect quality; supplier boundary is currently vague. |
| AB-08 Item Identification | Conditional | Needed only if Ekonerg controls traceable physical items, samples, or other identified deliverables. |
| AB-09 Special Processes | Conditional | Needed only if Ekonerg performs or controls welding, heat treatment, NDE, or another special process. |
| AB-10 Inspection | Applicable | Independent review or inspection of engineering outputs is part of quality control. |
| AB-11 Test Control | Conditional | Applies if Ekonerg performs or accepts tests; confirm through project samples. |
| AB-12 Measuring Equipment | Conditional | Applies if calibrated measurement or test devices are used for covered work. |
| AB-13 Handling | Conditional | Applies if physical items or sensitive deliverables need handling, storage, preservation, or shipment controls. |
| AB-14 Status | Conditional | Applies where inspection, test, or operating status must prevent wrong use or release. |
| AB-15 Nonconforming Items | Applicable | Owner confirmed Part 21 relevance; Ekonerg's nonconformance process is directly in scope. |
| AB-16 Corrective Action | Applicable | Owner confirmed Part 21 relevance; cause, action, reporting, and follow-up are directly in scope. |
| AB-17 QA Records | Applicable | Design, consultancy, nonconformance, and corrective-action records must be retained and retrievable. |
| AB-18 Audits | Applicable | Audit and management-review controls are part of the QA-system assessment. |

### Applicability result

The preliminary screen has **12 applicable** and **6 conditional** criteria;
it has **0 final N/A** criteria. A conditional criterion may become N/A only
after a work-scope check records the fact, the reason, and owner approval.

## 6. Required evidence next

1. Select representative design, engineering, and consultancy work samples.
2. Identify any physical items, tests, measuring devices, special processes,
   or status controls used in those samples.
3. Obtain the supplier/subcontractor list and one quality-relevant flow-down
   sample, if such suppliers exist.
4. Trace a nonconformance and a corrective action under the Part 21 scope.
5. Keep all results draft until the evidence-acceptance gate is recorded.
