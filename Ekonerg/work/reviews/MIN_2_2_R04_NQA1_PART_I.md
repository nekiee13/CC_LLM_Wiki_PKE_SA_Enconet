# MIN-2.2 R-04 — ASME NQA-1 Part I

## What and why

This batch processes ASME NQA-1 Part I (`DOC-0004`). Under the owner's scope
decision, Part I is the mandatory NQA-1 interpretation baseline. It is used to
explain what good Appendix B controls look like; it is not a separate
regulation replacing Appendix B.

## Boundary and applicability

Part I requirements are the mandatory interpretation baseline, but the later
audit still must decide which controls fit Ekonerg's actual design,
engineering-service, and consultancy work. A Part I statement is not by itself
proof that Ekonerg has a matching control or that every nuclear-facility
scenario applies.

Part II, Part III, and Part IV remain separate streams. This batch does not
make Part II mandatory and does not use guidance text as a mandatory rule.
