## Standing technical notes

- **Reuse axiom (owner directive, 2026-09-29):** future company bootstrapping must use
  the versioned, company-neutral `audit_template/` bundles (hash-pinned manifest,
  guarded preview/apply, journaled) rather than repeated per-file patches. Project
  scripts remain local copies per project.
- **Appendix B / ASME NQA-1 selection is not a neutrality defect.** The owner confirmed
  (2026-09-30) that assessing a company's QA system against 10 CFR 50 Appendix B, using
  ASME NQA-1 Part 1 as the mandatory interpretation baseline, is the shared audit
  question across Enconet, Ekonerg, IBE, IMK, TEKOL, and IGH. NQA-1 Part 2 is not
  mandatory unless explicitly invoked by contract or approved scope; Parts 3/4 are
  guidance only. Do not treat default Appendix B/NQA-1 Part 1 wiring as a company-leak.
- **Owner scope decision (2026-10-02):** Ekonerg's in-scope activities are design,
  engineering services, and consultancy; Ekonerg is the sole supplier in this audit's
  scope (supplier/subcontractor boundary still vague — remains an open item); Part 21
  applies mainly to nonconformance/corrective-action controls. See
  `docs/reviews/EF_3_1_OWNER_SCOPE_DECISION.md` for the full preliminary Appendix B
  applicability screen (12 applicable / 6 conditional / 0 final N/A).
- **`scripts/reset_audit.py` is guarded but real**: preview is the default; `--apply`
  requires an externally-stored, hash-checked plan, an external ZIP backup, and the
  exact token `RESET-EKONERG`. Never run `--apply` without the owner's explicit
  confirmation and a reviewed plan in hand.
- **Known gap for MIN-1.1:** `db/schema.sql`'s `criteria` table has no seeding
  mechanism yet. `evaluation_engine.write_rulings` requires exactly 18 criteria rows,
  and `crumbs.criterion_id` is a `NOT NULL` foreign key — both will fail against a
  freshly `init_db.py`-created database until a criteria-seeding step is added. Flag
  this before any MIN-1.2 synthetic rehearsal or real MIN-2 work.
