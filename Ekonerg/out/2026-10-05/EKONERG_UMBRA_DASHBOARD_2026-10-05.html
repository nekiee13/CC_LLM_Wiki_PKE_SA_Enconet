Executive Summary

TEKOL d.d. shows a substantially developed quality assurance system for NEK Krško surface-protection work. The strongest areas are organization, document control, special-process control, traceability, nonconformance control, corrective action, measuring-equipment control, and audits.

The main conformance risk is not absence of a QA system. The main risk is incomplete Appendix B / NQA-1-style evidence in design control, procurement QA flow-down, test control, and QA record-retention detail.

Primary residual risk: Criteria III, IV, XI, and XVII contain important evidence gaps. Remediation should focus on independent design verification, procurement clauses, test-program definition, and the QA records matrix.
Suitability statement: The documented system supports continued NEK contractor-scope coating work, subject to verification of lower-tier procedures and project records.

Top Gaps Requiring Remediation

  • Criterion III — Design Control: independent verification, design-interface control, design-basis translation, and deviation control need stronger direct evidence.
  • Criterion IV — Procurement Document Control: Appendix B, regulatory, design-basis, right-of-access, records, and supplier-QA-program flow-down should be added or sampled in procurement templates.
  • Criterion XI — Test Control: written test program structure should define prerequisites, instruments, environmental conditions, acceptance limits, result documentation, and evaluation.
  • Criterion XVII — QA Records: a records matrix should list type, owner, storage location, retention duration, retrieval method, protection method, and disposal rules.

Priority Auditor Verification Actions

  1. Verify organizational independence and direct reporting of QA personnel.
  2. Review the full Appendix B compliance matrix and current document index.
  3. Sample design packages and verify independent design review.
  4. Check NEK approvals for SR/AQ technological specifications and changes.
