# Crumb quality

Read the local `sieving/SIEVING_PLAYBOOK.md`, the approved source chunk, and
the crumb. Require one atomic claim with a verbatim quote and exact locator.
Keep the quote language (`sl`, `en`, or `hr`); do not translate evidence.
RULE can cite approved governing or interpretive authority. DOCUMENT must
not acquire normative authority from a company QA document.

Check these recurring Appendix B confusions against the source text:

- V / VI / XVII: work instructions and acceptance criteria; document issue
  and change control; completed quality records.
- IV / VII: procurement-document requirements; evaluation or evidence of
  supplier and item conformance.
- X / XI: independent inspection; planned performance testing.

Reject or split a crumb when its claim spans unrelated controls, the quote
only implies it, or the item type is unsupported. Do not classify from a
translation alone. Link any recurring lesson to the local prompt history.
